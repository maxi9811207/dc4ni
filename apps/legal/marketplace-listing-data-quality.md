## Full Description

### The Problem
Dirty CRM data costs your team hours every week. Duplicate contacts inflate your pipeline forecasts by 12-25%, cause embarrassing automation misfires, and erode trust in your CRM.

HubSpot's built-in deduplication is basic (manual, one-by-one merging), and the advanced data quality tools in Operations Hub Pro cost $720/month.

### The Solution
Data Quality Pro gives you powerful data hygiene tools at a fraction of the cost:

**Duplicate Scanner**
- Fuzzy matching on email, phone, name, and company
- Confidence scoring (high/medium) so you review the most likely duplicates first
- One-click merge directly from the results

**Data Cleanup**
- Automatic name casing (john doe to John Doe)
- Email standardization (trim whitespace, lowercase)
- Phone number normalization
- Preview mode — see all changes before applying

**CRM Health Score**
- Overall score out of 100
- Category breakdown: Completeness, Consistency, Duplicate Risk, Lifecycle Freshness
- Specific recommendations for improvement

### Why Data Quality Pro?
- Runs entirely inside HubSpot (no external data transfer)
- Native UI that feels like part of HubSpot
- Set it and forget it — scheduled scans coming soon
- 10x cheaper than upgrading to Operations Hub Pro

### Pricing
- **Free:** Health Score + limited duplicate scan (up to 100 contacts)
- **Starter ($19/mo):** Full duplicate scan + data cleanup preview
- **Pro ($49/mo):** Unlimited scans, one-click merge, auto-cleanup, scheduled runs
