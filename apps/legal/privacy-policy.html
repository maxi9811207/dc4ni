Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Data Quality Pro and Portal Audit ("we", "our", or "the Apps") collect, use, and protect information when you use our HubSpot applications.

1. Information We Access

Our Apps access the following HubSpot data through authorized API scopes:

  • Portal Audit: Contact, Company, and Deal records (read-only); Forms; Lists; Workflows; Properties.
  • Data Quality Pro: Contact and Company records (read and write for cleanup/merge operations); Property schemas.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the app's functionality:

  • Scanning for duplicate records
  • Analyzing data quality issues
  • Merging duplicate contacts (only when explicitly triggered by you)
  • Standardizing data formats (only when explicitly triggered by you)
  • Mapping portal asset dependencies
  • Generating health scores and audit reports

3. Data Storage

Our Apps do not store, export, or transfer your HubSpot data outside of HubSpot. All processing occurs within HubSpot's serverless function infrastructure. No data is sent to external servers.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer your data to any third parties. Period.

5. Data Retention

Since we do not store your data externally, there is no data retention outside of HubSpot. When you uninstall the app, all access to your data is immediately revoked.

6. Security

Our Apps use HubSpot's OAuth 2.0 authentication and run entirely within HubSpot's secure infrastructure. We follow HubSpot's security best practices for app development.

7. GDPR Compliance

Our Apps are designed to be GDPR-compliant:

  • We process data only as necessary to provide app functionality
  • We do not store personal data outside of HubSpot
  • You can revoke access at any time by uninstalling the app
  • We do not use your data for profiling or automated decision-making

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Know what data our apps access (described above)
  • Revoke access at any time by uninstalling the app
  • Request information about how your data is processed

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes through the HubSpot App Marketplace.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the HubSpot App Marketplace support channel.
